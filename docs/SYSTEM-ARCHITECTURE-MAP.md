# SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE MAP

## Purpose

DAA is organized as a sequence of architectural layers.

Each layer answers a fundamental question.

Each layer depends on the previous one.

Each layer preserves the meaning established below while expanding the system's capabilities.

---

# Architectural Flow

```text
Grammar
    ↓
Kernel
    ↓
Coherence
    ↓
Knowledge
    ↓
Core
    ↓
Evolution
    ↓
Configuration
    ↓
Domains
```

---

# Layers

## Grammar

**Question**

What does it mean?

Grammar defines the language of the ecosystem.

It establishes shared meaning.

Nothing below Grammar exists.

---

## Kernel

**Question**

What preserves meaning?

Kernel defines the fundamental architectural structures.

It establishes concepts, relationships, laws and guardians that preserve system identity.

---

## Coherence

**Question**

How is identity preserved while the system changes?

Coherence maintains integrity.

It continuously evaluates whether change preserves architectural identity.

---

## Knowledge

**Question**

How does the system learn?

Knowledge transforms observation into accumulated capability.

Experience becomes learning.

Learning becomes reusable knowledge.

---

## Core

**Question**

How do architectural capabilities operate together?

Core coordinates the capabilities provided by previous layers.

It introduces no new meaning.

It orchestrates coherent behavior.

---

## Evolution

**Question**

How do new possibilities emerge?

Evolution transforms potential into possibilities.

Coherent possibilities become emergent capabilities.

---

## Configuration

**Question**

How are capabilities composed?

Configuration organizes technologies, modules, missions and constraints into coherent system instances.

It transforms architectural capability into configurable solutions.

---

## Domains

**Question**

How is architecture expressed?

Domains apply architectural capabilities within specific operational contexts.

They represent real-world expressions of the system.

---

# Dependency Principle

Each layer may consume capabilities from previous layers.

No layer may redefine the meaning established below.

```text
Meaning
    ↓
Structure
    ↓
Integrity
    ↓
Learning
    ↓
Coordination
    ↓
Evolution
    ↓
Configuration
    ↓
Operational Expression
```

---

# Architectural Principles

Meaning precedes structure.

Structure preserves identity.

Identity enables coherent evolution.

Knowledge expands adaptive capability.

Core coordinates capabilities.

Configuration composes solutions.

Domains express architecture.

---

# Design Philosophy

DAA is not organized around objects.

It is organized around architectural capabilities.

Each package represents a capability.

The architecture emerges from the coherent interaction between capabilities rather than from isolated components.

---

# System Vision

Meaning creates structure.

Structure preserves coherence.

Coherence enables learning.

Learning expands capability.

Capability enables evolution.

Evolution generates configurations.

Configurations become operational systems.

The architecture remains coherent while continuously adapting.
