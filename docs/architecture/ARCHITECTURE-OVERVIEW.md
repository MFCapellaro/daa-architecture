# Architecture Overview

## Purpose

The DAA architecture is organized as a sequence of conceptual layers.

Each layer answers a fundamental architectural question, builds upon the previous one, and preserves the Grammar that gives the ecosystem its identity.

Meaning precedes mechanism.

---

## Architectural Vision

DAA is designed as a living architecture.

Rather than organizing software around implementation details, it organizes the system around meaning, relationships and coherent evolution.

Each architectural layer introduces new capabilities while preserving the coherence established by previous layers.

---

## Architectural Layers

### Grammar

**Question**

What does it mean?

**Purpose**

Grammar defines the shared language of the ecosystem.

It establishes meaning before implementation.

---

### Kernel

**Question**

What principles never change?

**Purpose**

Kernel defines the foundational concepts, laws and relationships that preserve the identity of the architecture.

---

### Core

**Question**

How is coherence organized?

**Purpose**

Core provides the structural mechanisms that coordinate relationships while preserving architectural continuity.

---

### Coherence

**Question**

How is identity preserved?

**Purpose**

Coherence maintains identity through balanced relationships.

Integrity emerges when identity is preserved across transformation.

---

### Knowledge

**Question**

How does the system learn?

**Purpose**

Knowledge transforms experience into coherent capacity.

Experience becomes observation.

Observations become learning.

Learning becomes knowledge.

---

### Evolution

**Question**

How does the system evolve?

**Purpose**

Evolution deploys coherent potential through time.

Each coherent state enables new possibilities while preserving identity.

---

### Configuration

**Question**

How is a specific system described?

**Purpose**

Configuration defines the concrete composition of a system.

It adapts architectural capabilities to particular domains without modifying the architecture itself.

---

### Domains

**Question**

How is the architecture applied?

**Purpose**

Domains specialize the architecture for real-world contexts while preserving the architectural principles defined by DAA.

---

## Architectural Relationships

The architecture evolves through coherent causal relationships.

Each layer provides the foundation required by the next.

```text
Grammar
    ↓
Kernel
    ↓
Core
    ↓
Coherence
    ↓
Knowledge
    ↓
Evolution
    ↓
Configuration
    ↓
Domains
```

---

## Construction Logic

Every package is developed following the Architectural Sequential Logic.

```text
Meaning
    ↓
Purpose
    ↓
Concepts
    ↓
Relationships
    ↓
Public API
    ↓
Validation
    ↓
Behaviors
```

Behavior never precedes conceptual validation.

---

## Architectural Principles

- Meaning precedes mechanism.
- Relationships precede behaviors.
- Identity is preserved through coherence.
- Knowledge transforms experience into coherent capacity.
- Evolution is the coherent causal deployment of potential through time.
- Configuration adapts architecture without changing identity.
- Domains specialize capabilities while preserving architectural principles.

---

## Summary

DAA is not organized around software components.

It is organized around the progressive construction of coherent meaning.

As coherence increases, new capabilities emerge without compromising identity.
