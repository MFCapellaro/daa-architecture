# LUX Precedence Laws

## Purpose

Precedence Laws define the order of conditions through which condensed coherence becomes recognizable and transmissible.

Precedence establishes the conditions from which coherent emergence can occur.

---

## Attention precedes Intention

Attention is the condition through which recognized coherence becomes meaningful.

Intention emerges when understanding aligns with meaningful coherence.

```text
Attention
    ↓
Intention
```

---

## Intention precedes Trajectory

Intention provides direction to coherent movement.

Trajectory represents the continuity of intentional evolution through time.

```text
Intention
    ↓
Trajectory
```

---

## Trajectory precedes Propagation

Trajectory preserves continuity through transformation.

Propagation emerges when coherent trajectory becomes transmissible across contexts.

```text
Trajectory
    ↓
Propagation
```

---

## Generative Relationship

Precedence enables emergence.

Emergence reveals the coherence of preceding conditions.

```text
Precedence
    ↓
Emergence
    ↺
Recognition
```

A coherent system evolves through the reciprocal relationship between preserved conditions and emerging realities.
