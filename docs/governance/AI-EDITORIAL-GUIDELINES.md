# AI Editorial Guidelines

## Purpose

These guidelines help AI assistants preserve the editorial consistency of DAA.

They complement the constitutional documents, the Grammar Concept Standard, and the Grammar Concept Template.

## Editorial Principles

When refining DAA documentation:

- Follow the established editorial pattern.
- Define one semantic primitive only.
- Preserve the same level of abstraction across related concepts.
- Avoid self-referential definitions.
- Preserve implementation independence.
- Prefer conceptual precision over descriptive completeness.
- Do not introduce new architectural meaning unless explicitly requested.

## Grammar Concepts

Grammar concepts should:

- expose only their semantic identity,
- express meaning through documentation,
- remain minimal and implementation-independent,
- preserve semantic continuity across the architecture.

These guidelines exist to help AI assistants preserve the editorial voice of DAA as the architecture evolves.
