# Trajectory Model

## Purpose

A trajectory preserves the continuity of identity transformations through time.

A system understands identity through the relational history that connects those states.

---

## Core Principle

Identity is revealed through the continuity of its coherent transformations.

A trajectory preserves the relational history from which coherence, trust and reputation can emerge.

---

## Relational Emergence

Static states become observable structures when connected through coherent temporal relationships.

A system reveals structure through the patterns generated by its transformations over time.

---

## Event → Signal → Pattern

A relational event is a temporal occurrence.

A trajectory signal is an observable consequence emerging from that event.

A pattern emerges from accumulated signals across time.

---

## Preservation Before Interpretation

Trajectories preserve events and signals before interpretation.

Meaning, coherence and reputation emerge from accumulated relational history.
