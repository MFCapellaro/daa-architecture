# Core Foundation

## Generative Structure

The Primary Triad is the minimal underlying structure that sustains evolutionary behavior.
It emerges from the dynamic relationship between:

- Sense
- Coherence
- Possibility

Sense provides direction.

Coherence preserves identity.

Possibility enables evolution.

Their dynamic balance allows systems to organize possibilities while maintaining coherence and meaning.

## Purpose

Core is the structural layer of Digital Adaptive Architecture.

It organizes the semantic possibilities defined by Grammar into coherent structures that can be composed, extended, and expressed by higher layers.

## Guiding Question

**How is meaning organized?**

## Relationship with Grammar

Core consumes Grammar as its semantic foundation.

It does not introduce new meaning or redefine foundational concepts. It transforms semantic primitives into structural compositions while preserving their identity, purpose, context, and relationships.

Core concepts are structural primitives that emerge from semantic primitives defined by Grammar.

They organize meaning while preserving semantic integrity.

## Structural Composition

Core defines the mechanisms that allow concepts to be organized into coherent architectural structures.

Where Grammar establishes meaning, Core establishes organization.

Structural composition enables semantic elements to maintain coherence as they are combined into larger systems.

## Structural Integrity

Structural integrity is revealed through the coherence between meaning, relationships, and behavior.

Core structures preserve semantic integrity by organizing meaning in ways that allow coherent behavior to emerge.

## Relationship with Higher Layers

Higher layers derive from Core structures to express domain-specific implementations and behaviours.

Core provides the structural conditions that allow meaning to become organized action while preserving continuity from semantic foundation to concrete expression.
