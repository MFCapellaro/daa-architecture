# Generation Architecture

## Purpose

Generation defines the architectural conditions through which coherent trajectories become collective capability.

Potential does not describe what a system is.

Potential describes latent capability revealed through internal or external trajectory.

---

## Architectural Role

Potential is the initial condition through which coherent interaction becomes possible.

It transforms individual possibility into the conditions required for collective emergence.

Potential does not create potency.

Interaction reveals potency.

---

Each condition preserves the identity of the preceding one while enabling the next.

---

## Two Sources of Potential

### External Potential

External Potential is recognized through ecosystem observation.

Its validation emerges from:

* context,
* public evidence,
* observable relationships,
* recognized experience.

External Potential signals coherent opportunities.

It enables invitation.

---

### Internal Potential

Internal Potential is revealed through coherent trajectory.

Its validation emerges from:

* purpose,
* contribution,
* coherent relationships,
* sustained evolution.

Internal Potential enables coherent interaction.

---

## Relationship with Trajectory

Trajectory preserves identity through coherent evolution.

Potential is revealed by trajectory.

```text
Trajectory
    reveals
Potential
```

Potential exists within Actors and Participants.

Trajectory reveals potential through coherent evolution.

---

## Relationship with Interaction

Interaction establishes coherent relationships between compatible potentials.

```text
Potential
    ↓
Possibility
    ↓
Compatibility
    ↓
Interaction
```

Interaction is the condition through which collective potency becomes possible.

---

## Relationship with Potency

Potency is not contained within an individual.

Potency emerges through coherent interaction among individual potentials sharing purpose.

```text
Potential
        +
Potential
        +
Purpose
        ↓
Interaction
        ↓
Potency
```

Collective potency is an emergent property of coherent relationships.
