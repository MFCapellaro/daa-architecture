# Potency

## Purpose

Potency enables collective capability to emerge through coherent interaction.

Potency is not an individual property.

It emerges from relationships that preserve coherence.

---

## Responsibility

Potency:

- emerges from coherent interaction,
- reveals collective capability,
- preserves the contribution of every participant,
- enables capability to emerge.

Potency does not belong to individuals.

Potency belongs to coherent relationships.

---

## Concepts

The fundamental concepts are:

- Interaction
- Relationship
- Coherence
- Emergence
- Capability

---

## Relationship within Generation

Potential reveals possibilities.

Compatibility recognizes coherent alignment.

Interaction establishes coherent relationships.

Potency emerges from coherent interaction.

Emergence reveals collective capability.

Capability becomes observable through emergence.

Collective potency belongs to the relationship.
