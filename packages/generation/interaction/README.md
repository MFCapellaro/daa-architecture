# Interaction

## Purpose

Interaction establishes coherent relationships between compatible potentials.

Interaction enables individual possibilities to become collective conditions.

Without coherent interaction, collective potency cannot emerge.

---

## Responsibility

Interaction:

- connects compatible potentials,
- establishes coherent relationships,
- preserves the identity of every participant,
- enables the emergence of collective potency.

Interaction does not create potential.

Interaction reveals the conditions through which potential becomes collective capability.

---

## Concepts

The fundamental concepts are:

- Compatibility
- Relationship
- Coherence
- Potential
- Potency

---

## Relationship within Generation

Generation reveals potential.

Compatibility recognizes coherent alignment.

Interaction establishes coherent relationships.

Potency emerges from interaction.

Capability emerges from collective potency.

---

## Core Principle

Interaction transforms compatible potential into coherent relationships.

Coherent relationships reveal collective potency.

Collective potency enables emergent capability.
