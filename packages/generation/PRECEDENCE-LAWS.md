# Generation Precedence Laws

## Purpose

Precedence Laws define the order of conditions through which generative capability emerges.

Precedence establishes the necessary conditions for coherent emergence.

---

## Identity precedes Coherence

Identity provides continuity.

Coherence preserves the relationships that maintain identity through transformation.

```text
Identity
    ↓
Coherence
```

---

## Coherence precedes Discernment

Discernment identifies coherent directions of evolution.

Trajectory provides continuity through purposeful movement.

```text
Coherence
    ↓
Discernment
```

---

## Discernment precedes Trajectory

Discernment identifies coherent directions of evolution.

Trajectory provides continuity through purposeful movement.

```text
Discernment
    ↓
Trajectory
```

---

## Trajectory reveals Potential

Trajectory does not create potential.

Trajectory reveals latent capability existing within Actors and Participants.

```text
Trajectory
    ↓
Potential
```

---

## Potential precedes Possibility

Potential becomes possibility when conditions and purpose allow its expression.

```text
Potential
    ↓
Possibility
```

---

## Possibility precedes Compatibility

Possibility establishes a context where different potentials can be evaluated for coherent alignment.

```text
Possibility
    ↓
Compatibility
```

---

## Compatibility precedes Interaction

Compatibility is the condition required for coherent relationships.

Interaction begins when compatible potentials actively relate.

```text
Compatibility
    ↓
Interaction
```

---

## Interaction precedes Potency

Potency does not exist within an individual.

Potency emerges from coherent interaction between potentials sharing purpose.

```text
Interaction
    ↓
Potency
```

---

## Potency precedes Emergence

Potency contains the capacity for new expression.

Emergence makes that expression observable.

```text
Potency
    ↓
Emergence
```

---

## Emergence precedes Capability

Capability is the observable and usable expression of emergent potency.

```text
Emergence
    ↓
Capability
```

---

## Capability precedes Knowledge

Knowledge integrates manifested capability into system memory.

```text
Capability
    ↓
Knowledge
```
