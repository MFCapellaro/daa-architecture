# Potential

## Identity

Potential represents the coherent possibilities revealed by a trajectory.

Potential is not a resource, a capability, or a prediction.

Potential is a condition revealed through coherent evolution.

---

## Purpose

The purpose of Potential is to reveal the conditions under which coherent interactions may generate collective potency.

Potential precedes interaction.

Interaction reveals potency.

---

## Responsibility

Potential identifies possibilities emerging from coherent trajectories.

It:

* reveals individual potential,
* enables compatibility between trajectories,
* prepares the conditions for coherent interaction,
* makes emergent potency possible.

Potential does not produce results.

It establishes the conditions from which results may emerge.

---

## Two Expressions of Potential

### External Potential

External Potential is revealed through ecosystem observation.

Its validation emerges from context, evidence, experience, and publicly observable relationships.

External Potential signals an opportunity for coherent relationship.

It may lead to invitation.

---

### Internal Potential

Internal Potential is revealed through coherent trajectory.

Its validation emerges from purpose, relationships, contributions, and sustained coherence within DAA.

Internal Potential enables coherent interaction.

## Precedence Laws

Trajectory precedes Potential.

Potential precedes Interaction.

Interaction precedes Potency.

Potency precedes Emergence.

Emergence precedes Capability.

---

## Core Principle

Individual Potential belongs to a Trajectory.

Collective Potency belongs to Interaction.

The value emerges from the coherent organization of individual potentials around a shared purpose.

Potential reveals possibility.

Interaction reveals potency.

---

## Relationship with Evolution

Potential belongs to the generative process.

Evolution does not create Potential.

Evolution integrates the knowledge generated through manifested potency into an expanded identity.

Potential prepares the future.

Evolution preserves what has become part of identity.
