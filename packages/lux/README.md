# LUX

## Purpose

LUX defines the coherent propagation mechanism through which expanded identity returns to the ecosystem.

LUX preserves recognition across contexts while enabling coherent relationships to expand.

LUX does not create capability.

LUX propagates capability that has emerged through coherent evolution.

---

## Responsibility

LUX defines the conditions where coherent identity becomes transmissible.

It:

* recognizes coherent patterns within contexts,
* preserves coherence during transmission,
* enables propagation across different contexts,
* extends relationships without losing identity.

LUX transforms expanded identity into ecosystem coherence.

---

## Identity

LUX is the propagation layer of DAA.

It connects evolved identity with new contexts.

It enables coherence to move without fragmentation.
