# Kernel Constitution

## Purpose

The Kernel Constitution defines the fundamental principles that govern every DAA system.

It does not describe implementations.

It defines the immutable conditions that allow coherent adaptive behavior to emerge while preserving identity.

---

# Constitutional Principles

## 1. Identity

A system exists through its identity.

Identity provides continuity across transformation.

A system is preserved through identity and coherence.

---

## 2. Meaning

Meaning gives purpose to every concept.

Meaning is the origin of coherent relationships.

Relationships without coherence are meaningless.

---

## 3. Relationships

Relationships connect concepts into coherent structures.

Relationships are the fundamental source of architectural behavior.

Structure emerges from coherent relationships.

---

## 4. Coherence

Coherence is the dynamic equilibrium of relationships.

It transforms relational potential into dynamic potency.

Coherence preserves identity while enabling adaptation.

---

## 5. Guardians

Guardians preserve constitutional coherence.

They continuously observe, interpret and align the system according to constitutional laws.

Together, Guardians form the adaptive sensory system of DAA.

---

# Precedence Laws

Precedence Laws describe the conditions required for new capabilities to emerge.

Meaning precedes Relationship.

Relationships precede Potency.

Potency precedes Movement.

Every new capability emerges from the coherent preservation of the previous one.

---

# Preservation Laws

Preservation Laws describe the conditions that maintain continuity during change.

Identity precedes Coherence.

Coherence enables Adaptation.

Adaptation preserves Identity.

A system adapts continuously while preserving its constitutional integrity.

---

# Emergent Phenomena

The Kernel preserves the conditions from which emergence naturally occurs.

The following phenomena are emergent:

- Potency
- Movement
- Trajectory
- Advance
- Knowledge
- Evolution

Emergent phenomena arise from coherent interaction.
