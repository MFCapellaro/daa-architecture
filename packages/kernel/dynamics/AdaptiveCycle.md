# AdaptiveCycle

## Purpose

AdaptiveCycle coordinates the dynamic flow from
relationship evolution to adaptive response.

It represents the complete mechanism through
which a system evaluates change and generates
coherent adaptation.

## Role in Dynamics

AdaptiveCycle integrates the complete dynamic
sequence:

RelationshipTrajectory
↓
CoherenceEvaluator
↓
CoherenceAssessment
↓
AdaptiveAction


## Principle

Adaptation is a coherent cycle, not an isolated
reaction.

A system evolves by continuously observing,
assessing and responding to changes while
preserving meaningful relationships.

## Responsibility

AdaptiveCycle:

- receives a relationship trajectory
- evaluates its coherence state
- generates an adaptive action

It does not:

- define system identity
- modify constitutional laws
- execute external operations

## Dynamic Flow

Transformation

  ↓

Relationship History

  ↓

Coherence Evaluation

  ↓

Assessment

  ↓

Adaptive Response

  ↓

Evolution


## Architectural Meaning

AdaptiveCycle is the operational expression of
coherent evolution.

It connects stability and change:

Identity Preservation
+
Dynamic Adaptation
=
Coherent Evolution


The cycle allows a system to transform while
maintaining continuity of meaning and identity.
