# CoherenceEvaluator

## Purpose

CoherenceEvaluator analyzes a relationship
trajectory to determine whether its evolution
preserves coherent continuity.

## Role in Dynamics

The evaluator transforms relational history
into a meaningful coherence assessment.

RelationshipTrajectory
↓
CoherenceEvaluator
↓
CoherenceAssessment


## Principle

A transformation is coherent when change
preserves the relationships that maintain
system identity.

## Responsibility

CoherenceEvaluator:

- observes trajectory evolution
- identifies coherence conditions
- produces a coherence assessment

It does not modify relationships.
It does not execute actions.

## Relationship

RelationshipTrajectory
↓
CoherenceEvaluator
↓
CoherenceAssessment
↓
AdaptiveAction


## Architectural Meaning

Evaluation creates understanding before action.

A system adapts coherently when it first
recognizes the state of its own evolution.

Observation
↓
Assessment
↓
Adaptation
