# RelationshipAdjustment

## Purpose

RelationshipAdjustment represents a transformation
between two relational states.

It describes how a relationship changes while
maintaining traceability of its evolution.

## Role in Dynamics

RelationshipAdjustment is the smallest unit
of dynamic transformation.

It connects:

Previous Relationship
        ↓
Transformation
        ↓
Current Relationship


## Principle

A system evolves by adjusting relationships,
not by losing them.

Change becomes meaningful when the transition
between states preserves coherence.


## Usage

RelationshipAdjustment is used by
RelationshipTrajectory to record relational
evolution over time.


## Relationship

RelationshipAdjustment
↓
RelationshipTrajectory
↓
CoherenceEvaluation


## Architectural Meaning

An adjustment is not a replacement.

It is evidence of continuity through change.
