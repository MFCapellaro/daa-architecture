# KernelRelationship

## Purpose

KernelRelationship defines the semantic connection
between two Kernel Concepts.

Relationships are the fundamental structural units
of the DAA Kernel.

A concept gains operational meaning when it enters
into a coherent relationship with other concepts.

---

## Principle

A system is not only composed of entities.

A system emerges from the relationships that organize
entities into coherent structures.

Relationships provide the mechanism through which:

- Meaning becomes structure.
- Concepts interact.
- Identity is preserved through change.
- Coherence can be evaluated.

---

## Structure

A KernelRelationship contains:

- Source Concept
- Semantic Verb
- Target Concept

The relationship identity is derived from:
source : verb : target


This provides a stable semantic reference
through transformations.

---

## Relationship Model
Concept
↓
Relationship
↓
Structure
↓
System Coherence


---

## Dynamic Role

Relationships are not static connections.

They can transform while preserving coherence.

During system adaptation:

Previous Relationship
↓
Relationship Adjustment
↓
Current Relationship


The trajectory of relationships represents
the movement of the system.

---

## DAA Principle

Coherence does not eliminate variability.

Coherence transforms variability into movement
that preserves identity.

Identity
↓
Relationships
↓
Coherent Movement
↓
Adaptive Continuity
