# Knowledge Flow

## Purpose

Knowledge Flow describes how distributed experience becomes collective knowledge within DAA.

It explains how coherent relationships transform observation into capability, and capability into expanded identity.

Knowledge is not created in isolation.

Knowledge emerges through coherent interaction and meaningful discernment.

---

# Fundamental Principle

Experience generates information.

Relationships reveal meaning.

Coherence transforms meaning into knowledge.

Knowledge enables capability.

Capability expands identity.

---

Each stage preserves the coherence of the previous one while enabling the next.

# Two Sources of Experience

Knowledge originates from two complementary domains.

## External Domain

The ecosystem provides observable experience.

```text
Ecosystem
        ↓
Observation
        ↓
Discovery
        ↓
Relationships
```

External observation reveals:

* opportunities,
* needs,
* tendencies,
* technologies,
* regulations,
* collective practices.

The ecosystem becomes a source of distributed knowledge.

---

## Internal Domain

Participants generate coherent experience through their trajectory.

```text
Participant
        ↓
Trajectory
        ↓
Potential
        ↓
Interaction
```

Internal experience reveals:

* potential,
* contribution,
* capability,
* collective potency.

---

# Convergence

Both domains converge through Generation.

```text
External Experience
        │
        ├──────────────┐
        │              │
Internal Experience    │
        │              │
        ▼              ▼
      Generation
            ↓
      Capability
            ↓
      Knowledge
```

Generation transforms distributed experience into coherent knowledge.

# Integration

Knowledge becomes stable only after integration.

```text
Knowledge
        ↓

        ↓
Adaptation
        ↓
Learning
        ↓
Evolution
        ↓
Expanded Identity
```

Evolution preserves the knowledge generated through coherent interaction.

Identity expands without losing continuity.

---

# Propagation

Expanded identity enables coherent propagation.

```text
Expanded Identity
        ↓
LUX
        ↓
Propagation
```

Knowledge returns to the ecosystem through:

* recommendations,
* improved practices,
* design improvements,
* collective intelligence,
* new relationships.

The ecosystem becomes more coherent through the knowledge it helped generate.
