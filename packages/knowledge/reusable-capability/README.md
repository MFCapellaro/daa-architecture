# Reusable Capability

## Identity

Reusable Capability is the preserved expression of knowledge that can participate in future generations.

It is not the original capability.

It is the capability integrated through learning.

---

## Purpose

Reusable Capability enables previously acquired knowledge to become the foundation of future coherent generation.

It preserves learning as actionable potential.

---

## Responsibility

Reusable Capability:

- preserves integrated capability,
- enables coherent reuse,
- supports future generation,
- expands evolutionary continuity.

Reusable Capability does not create new capability.

Generation creates capability.

Knowledge preserves it.

Evolution integrates it into identity.

---

## Architectural Relationships

```text
Capability
    ↓
Experience
    ↓
Learning
    ↓
Knowledge
    ↓
Reusable Capability
    ↓
```

---

## Core Principle

Capability becomes reusable only after coherent learning.

Reusable Capability preserves evolutionary continuity by allowing knowledge to participate in future generations.
