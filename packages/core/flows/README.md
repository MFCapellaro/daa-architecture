# Flows

## Purpose

Flows express coordinated behavior.

They transform coherent relationships into observable system dynamics.

Flows do not define meaning.

They do not preserve identity.

They express coordinated operation.

---

## Guiding Question

**How does coherence become behavior?**

---

## Architectural Role

Grammar defines meaning.

Kernel organizes relationships.

Coherence preserves integrity.

Knowledge integrates learning into reusable capability.

Core coordinates possibilities.

Relationships enable evolution.

Flows express coordinated behavior.

Legacy preserves validated understanding.

---

## Design Principles

Flows emerge from coherent relationships.

Every flow represents coordinated behavior.

Flows express evolution without redefining identity.

Only validated behaviors become Legacy.

---

## Evolution

Flows continuously adapt through coherent coordination.

Their quality depends on the relationships that generate them.

---

## Design Principle

Behavior is not programmed.

Behavior emerges from coherent relationships expressed through flows.
