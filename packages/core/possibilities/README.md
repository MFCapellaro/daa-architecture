# Possibilities

## Purpose

Possibilities coordinate the exploration of coherent evolution.

They do not define potential.

They organize the evaluation of potential while preserving system identity.

---

## Guiding Question

**What coherent evolution is possible?**

---

## Architectural Role

Grammar defines meaning.

Kernel organizes relationships.

Coherence preserves integrity.

Knowledge builds capability.

Core coordinates possibilities.

Relationships enable evolution.

Flows express coordinated behavior.

Legacy preserves validated understanding.

---

## Design Principles

Possibilities emerge from existing coherence.

Every possibility represents potential change.

Only coherent possibilities may become coordinated behavior.

Rejected possibilities preserve learning without altering identity.

---

## Evolution

The space of possibilities expands through accumulated knowledge.

The quality of possibilities depends on the coherence preserved by lower architectural layers.

---

## Purpose

Possibilities transform preserved potential into coordinated opportunities for evolution.
