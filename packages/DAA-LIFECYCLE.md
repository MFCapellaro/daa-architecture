# DAA Lifecycle

## Purpose

The DAA Lifecycle defines the coherent evolutionary spiral through which systems preserve identity while continuously expanding knowledge, capability and meaning.

The lifecycle is not a repetitive cycle.

It is a coherent spiral where every new generation begins from an enriched state.

---

## Identity

DAA evolves through continuous coherent transformation.

Each evolutionary cycle preserves identity while revealing it with greater clarity.

Identity is not replaced.

Identity becomes progressively more coherent through learning.

---

---

### Generation

---

### Experience

---

### Learning

Learning integrates coherent experience.

Learning transforms observation into reusable understanding.

---

### Knowledge

Knowledge preserves coherent learning.

Knowledge enables future generations to begin from expanded capability.

---

### Discernment

Discernment recognizes which transformations preserve coherence.

It provides evolutionary direction.

---

### Adaptation

Adaptation transforms capability while preserving identity.

---

### Evolution

Evolution expands capability through coherent adaptation.

Evolution preserves continuity while increasing evolutionary potential.

---

### Enriched Identity

Identity becomes progressively clearer through coherent learning.

---

### Deeper Meaning

Each evolutionary cycle deepens meaning.

Expanded meaning becomes the origin of the next generation.
