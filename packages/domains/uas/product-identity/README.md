# Product Identity

## Purpose

Product Identity represents the coherent identity of a product within the UAS ecosystem.

It organizes publicly available and verifiable information into a structured representation that allows products to be recognized, understood and compared.

Product Identity describes what a product is.

It does not define what a supplier offers.

---

## Responsibility

Product Identity:

* preserves product identity,
* organizes publicly available technical information,
* records information sources,
* preserves factual evidence,
* provides a coherent basis for product recognition,
* supports comparison and informed participation.

Product Identity does not contain private commercial agreements.

---

## Information Boundary

Product Identity is based primarily on publicly available information.

This may include:

* technical specifications,
* capabilities,
* applications,
* manufacturer information,
* documentation,
* certifications,
* publicly available technical conditions,
* source references.

Information is preserved together with its source and context whenever relevant.

---

## Market Reference

Commercial information may be observed as market reference when it contributes to product evaluation.

Market reference is not part of the public Product Identity unless its publication is authorized.

Commercial references must preserve their context, including when relevant:

* source,
* geographic location,
* date,
* currency,
* commercial conditions.

Market reference does not establish a DAA offer.

It provides internal evidence for evaluating future commercial proposals.

---

## Commercial Boundary

Commercial conditions begin when a supplier establishes an agreement with DAA.

```text
Public Information
        ↓
Product Identity
        ↓
Supplier Integration
        ↓
Agreement
        ↓
Pool Offer
        ↓
Pool
        ↓
Transaction
```

Product Identity describes the product.

The Agreement defines the supplier's offer.

The Pool exposes the resulting collective opportunity.

---

## Product Categories

The UAS Domain initially organizes Product Identity into:

```text
Product Identity

├── Batteries
├── Drones
├── Field Work Kits
├── Generators
├── Meteorology
└── Mixers
```

Additional categories may emerge from ecosystem observation.

---

## Evidence Principle

Product information becomes reliable knowledge when its factual basis can be recognized and evaluated.

Public information is therefore preserved together with its source whenever possible.

Convergence between independent sources increases evidential confidence.

Evidence must remain coherent with its context before becoming part of validated knowledge.

---

## Architectural Relationship

```text
Knowledge
    ↓
Product Identity
    ↓
Agreement
    ↓
Pool
    ↓
Collective Opportunity
```

Knowledge provides the informational substrate.

Product Identity organizes that substrate around a specific product.

Agreement introduces private commercial conditions.

Pool transforms the agreed conditions into a collective opportunity.

---

## Core Principle

Product Identity defines what the product is.

It does not define what the product costs, what a supplier offers, or what a Pool negotiates.

Public evidence belongs to Product Identity.

Private commercial conditions belong to Agreements and Pools.
