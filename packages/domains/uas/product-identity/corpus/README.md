# First Corpus

## Purpose

The First Corpus is the initial empirical dataset used by DAA to explore product reality within the UAS domain.

It preserves observations collected from existing products and their available sources before classification, normalization or structural interpretation.

The Corpus provides the factual substrate from which Product Classes and Characteristic Comparison Schemas may emerge.

---

## Role

The Corpus is an exploratory layer.

It does not define:

* Product Classes,
* Characteristic Schemas,
* Product Families,
* Product Models,
* commercial offers,
* supplier agreements.

These emerge from subsequent processes of convergence, coherence and discernment.

---

## Core Principle

The Corpus preserves what has been observed before deciding what it means structurally.

Observation precedes interpretation.

Evidence precedes validation.

Discernment precedes structure.

---

## Methodological Precedence

The transformation from public information into structured product knowledge follows a strict order of precedence.

```text
Source
  ↓
Observation
  ↓
Normalization
  ↓
Classification
  ↓
Discernment
  ↓
Product Class
  ↓
Characteristic Comparison Schema
```

Each stage preserves the coherence and traceability of the preceding stage.

### Source

The source establishes the origin of information.

Source identity, authority, context and accessibility are preserved.

### Observation

Observation records what is found in the source before structural interpretation.

Original terminology, value, unit and context are preserved whenever available.

### Normalization

Normalization establishes comparable representation without changing the factual content of the observation.

Normalization does not establish meaning beyond what is supported by the source.

### Classification

Classification organizes normalized observations according to emerging similarities and differences.

Classification is an organizational operation, not a final semantic judgment.

### Discernment

Discernment evaluates convergence, divergence, relevance and coherence across classified observations.

Discernment separates structural characteristics from incidental or contextual information.

### Product Class

A Product Class emerges when sufficient coherent evidence establishes a recurring structural identity across products.

Product Classes are discovered rather than imposed.

### Characteristic Comparison Schema

The Characteristic Comparison Schema emerges from the Product Class.

It defines which characteristics can be coherently compared between products belonging to the same class.

---

## Observation Integrity

The original observation must remain recoverable after normalization and classification.

```text
Raw Observation
      ↓
Normalized Representation
      ↓
Classification
      ↓
Discernment
```

No subsequent stage may overwrite the evidential origin of the information.

---

## Convergence

Convergence occurs when observations from different products, sources or contexts reveal sufficient semantic consistency to support further evaluation.

Convergence does not automatically establish validity.

Evidence must pass through coherence and discernment before becoming structural knowledge.

```text
Observations
     ↓
Convergence
     ↓
Evidence
     ↓
Coherence
     ↓
Discernment
```

---

## Evidence Status

Observations may have different evidential conditions.

The Corpus may preserve states such as:

```text
Observed
Corroborated
Convergent
Validated
Unresolved
```

These states describe the current evidential condition.

They do not constitute final Product Identity.

---

## Source Independence

Multiple sources from the same publisher do not constitute independent evidence.

The Corpus therefore distinguishes between:

```text
Same-Publisher Corroboration
        ↓
Independent Corroboration
        ↓
Convergent Evidence
```

The absence of independent corroboration does not invalidate an observation.

It establishes an evidential limitation that must remain visible.

---

## Product Observation Record

Observations are organized by product rather than by individual characteristic.

```text
Product Observation Record
│
├── Product Identity
├── Sources
├── Observations
├── Context
└── Evidential Status
```

This structure condenses related information without reducing its semantic content.

A single product record may contain multiple observations while preserving the identity, source and context of each observation.

---

## Observation Organization

Observations may be grouped into provisional categories for practical organization.

Possible categories include:

* Physical
* Operational
* Functional
* Performance
* Environmental
* Navigation
* Sensing
* Power
* Communication
* Other
* Unclassified

These categories are organizational containers.

They are not Product Classes and must not be interpreted as final schemas.

New categories may emerge when the Corpus reveals that existing categories are insufficient.

---

## Raw Terminology

Original terminology should be preserved whenever possible.

Different sources may describe related characteristics using different terms.

For example:

```text
Maximum Takeoff Weight
MTOW
Maximum Take-Off Mass
```

These remain distinct observations until semantic equivalence is established through the methodological process.

Normalization may associate them without erasing their original expression.

---

## Context

Product information may vary according to:

* geographic location,
* market,
* date,
* product version,
* configuration,
* operational condition,
* regulatory environment.

Context must therefore be preserved whenever it affects interpretation.

An observation without sufficient context must not be treated as universally applicable.

---

## Commercial Information

Publicly observable commercial information may be preserved when useful as internal market reference.

It is not automatically part of the public Product Identity.

Commercial information must retain its source and context.

Private supplier conditions belong to Agreements and Pools, not to the Corpus.

---

## Integrity Principle

The Corpus preserves the distinction between:

```text
Observed Fact
      ↓
Normalized Representation
      ↓
Classification
      ↓
Discernment
      ↓
Validated Knowledge
```

An interpretation must never overwrite the original observation.

The evidence trail must remain recoverable.

---

## Evolution

The First Corpus is not static.

New observations may:

* corroborate existing evidence,
* contradict previous observations,
* reveal missing characteristics,
* reveal new product classes,
* refine existing schemas,
* invalidate previous assumptions.

The Corpus therefore provides the empirical substrate for continuous refinement.

```text
New Observation
       ↓
Existing Evidence
       ↓
Convergence / Contradiction
       ↓
Discernment
       ↓
Refined Structure
```

---

## Core Principle

The First Corpus does not tell DAA what a product is.

It preserves enough evidence for DAA to discover it.

Reality provides the observations.

Sources establish origin.

Normalization establishes comparability.

Classification organizes convergence.

Discernment reveals structure.

Product Classes emerge from coherent evidence.

Characteristic Comparison Schemas emerge from Product Classes.

The resulting structure becomes Product Identity.
