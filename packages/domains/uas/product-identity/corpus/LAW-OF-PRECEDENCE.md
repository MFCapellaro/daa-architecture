# Law of Precedence

## Purpose

The Law of Precedence establishes that coherent meaning emerges through an ordered succession of conditions.

A later concept depends on conditions established by preceding concepts.

Precedence therefore does not merely establish sequence.

It establishes the conditions under which emergence becomes legitimate.

---

## Core Principle

> **A concept cannot legitimately emerge before the conditions that give it meaning have been established.**

Precedence preserves coherence across transformation.

```text
Precedence
    ↓
Conditions
    ↓
Coherence
    ↓
Emergence
```

---

# 1. Source Precedes Observation

A source establishes the origin from which information can be observed.

```text
Source
  ↓
Observation
```

Without a source, an observation has no established origin.

### Principle

> **Origin precedes observation.**

---

# 2. Observation Precedes Normalization

An observation preserves what has actually been encountered.

```text
Observation
  ↓
Normalization
```

Normalization must not precede the evidence it normalizes.

### Principle

> **Evidence precedes semantic unification.**

---

# 3. Normalization Precedes Convergence

Convergence requires a common semantic reference.

```text
Normalized Concepts
       ↓
Convergence
```

Without normalization, apparent convergence may simply be terminology similarity.

### Principle

> **Semantic coherence precedes convergence.**

---

# 4. Convergence Precedes Divergence

Divergence requires a shared reference.

```text
Convergence
     ↓
Shared Ground
     ↓
Divergence
```

Without convergence, a difference cannot yet be interpreted as a divergence.

### Principle

> **Common ground precedes meaningful difference.**

---

# 5. Convergence and Divergence Precede Discernment

Discernment requires both what remains common and what becomes distinct.

```text
          Convergence
               │
               ├──────┐
               │      │
               ▼      ▼
          Shared     Difference
          Ground       │
               │       │
               └───┬───┘
                   ▼
               Discernment
```

Discernment determines the meaningful distinction between observations, concepts, functions, and structures.

### Principle

> **Discernment emerges from coherent comparison of convergence and divergence.**

---

# 6. Discernment Precedes Function

A function is not simply a term found in a source.

It is a meaning discerned from observations, context, convergence, and divergence.

```text
Evidence
   ↓
Discernment
   ↓
Function
```

### Principle

> **Functional meaning follows functional discernment.**

### Principle

# 8. Functional Relationship Precedes Functional Composition

A composition is more than the presence of multiple functions.

```text
   ↓
```

# 9. Functional Composition Precedes Product Capability

Product Capability emerges when a coherent functional composition produces an observable operational ability.

```text
Functional Composition
```

Product Capability cannot be established solely by listing functions.

# Precedence Is Not Merely Temporal

Precedence does not necessarily mean that one event occurs earlier in physical time.

It means that one condition is **conceptually or structurally prior** to another.

For example:

```text
Function
    ↓
Relationship
```

does not mean that functions exist for a period of time before relationships occur.

It means that a relationship cannot be meaningfully defined without knowing what is being related.

Therefore:

> **Precedence describes dependency of meaning, not merely chronology.**

# Precedence and Evidence

The law protects the Corpus from inference running ahead of evidence.

```text

    ↓

    ↓

    ↓
Emergence
```

```text
Assumed Product Capability
    ↓
Invented Functions
    ↓
Selected Evidence
```

The Corpus must never use evidence merely to justify a conclusion established beforehand.

Classification organizes meanings that have already been sufficiently discerned.

It must not be used to manufacture those meanings.

> **Nothing meaningful may be established downstream before the conditions that make its meaning coherent have emerged upstream.**
