# Knowledge

## Purpose

Knowledge organizes relevant information within the UAS ecosystem into coherent understanding.

It transforms dispersed information into structured knowledge that enables recognition, relationships and future capabilities.

Knowledge does not create information.

It preserves, organizes and contextualizes existing information so the ecosystem can evolve coherently.

---

## Architectural Role

Knowledge is the first layer of interaction between reality and the UAS domain.

It observes existing information from:

- technologies,
- products,
- manufacturers,
- institutions,
- regulations,
- training,
- operational experiences.

It transforms these observations into structured knowledge that supports ecosystem relationships.

---

## Responsibility

Knowledge:

- preserves relevant information,
- organizes domain-specific understanding,
- provides context for ecosystem relationships,
- supports product identity definition,
- enables informed participation,
- captures learning generated by experience.

Knowledge does not replace specialized expertise.

It creates the structure through which expertise can become accessible.

Knowledge continuously evolves through interaction with the ecosystem.

```text

    ↓

    ↓
```

---

## Core Principle

Knowledge does not accumulate information.

Knowledge reveals coherence within information.

Through coherence, information becomes a foundation for collective capability.
