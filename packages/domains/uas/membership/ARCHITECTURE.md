# DAA UAS Membership Architecture

## Purpose

The Membership domain represents the transition from external ecosystem recognition to internal DAA participation.

Membership preserves voluntary belonging while enabling coherent participation.

---

# Fundamental Principle

> Recognition precedes belonging.

An Actor becomes a Participant only through voluntary integration.

---

---

# Core Concepts

## Participant

A Participant represents an identity belonging to DAA.

Participation enables:

* relationships,
* trajectories,
* contribution,
* evolution.

---

# Responsibilities

Membership is responsible for:

* preserving participation,
* maintaining participation status,
* enabling internal relationships.

It is not responsible for:

* discovering entities,
* recognizing actors,
* maintaining ecosystem information.

Those responsibilities belong to the Ecosystem domain.

---

# Relationship with Ecosystem

```text
Ecosystem

↓

Recognition

↓

Membership

↓

Participation
```

Membership is the bridge between external reality and internal evolution.

---

# Identity

Identity is never replaced.

Participation extends identity through coherent relationships.

```text
Identity

+

Belonging

↓

Contribution

↓

Evolution
```

---

# Guiding Statement

> Membership is the conscious decision to participate in coherent evolution.
