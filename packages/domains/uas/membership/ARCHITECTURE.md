# Participant Architecture

## Architectural Role

Participant is responsible for belonging.

It establishes that an Actor has become part of the DAA ecosystem.

Participant does not describe evolution.

Trajectory assumes that responsibility.

---

## Structural Position

```text
Ecosystem

↓

Membership

↓

Trajectory

↓

Revealed Potential

↓

Interaction

↓

Coordinated Potency
```

Membership is the architectural bridge between existence and coherent evolution.

---

## Core Relationships

```text
Actor
    becomes
Participant

Participant
    enables
Trajectory
```

Belonging precedes evolution.

Without participation there is no coherent trajectory.

Without trajectory there is no revealed potential.

---

## Architectural Responsibility

Participant establishes membership.

It does not:

* organize evolution,
* define capabilities,
* reveal potential,
* coordinate interactions,
* generate potency.

Its responsibility is complete once belonging has been established.

This preserves the Law of Precedence throughout the architecture.
