# DAA UAS Ecosystem Discovery Model

## Purpose

The Discovery Model defines how DAA observes and records existing entities from the external ecosystem.

Discovery is the first relationship between reality and system memory.

DAA does not create external entities.

DAA recognizes what already exists.

---

# Fundamental Principle

> Observe before transforming.

A dynamic system begins by understanding existing conditions, relationships and context before establishing structures that modify them.

---

# Discovery Flow

```text id="k7m3pz"
External Reality

↓

Observation

↓

ExternalEntity

↓

DiscoveryRecord

↓

Validation

↓

Actor Recognition
```

Discovery transforms unknown external reality into recognized ecosystem knowledge.

---

# Discovery Is Not Data Collection

A simple data record answers:

> What information do we have?

Discovery answers:

> What relationship does this entity have with the ecosystem?

Therefore Discovery preserves:

* origin,
* context,
* relationships,
* evidence,
* evolution.

---

# Discovery Sources

Information may emerge from different sources:

* public information,
* direct registration,
* recommendations,
* ecosystem references,
* institutional sources,
* operational experience.

A DiscoverySource preserves the origin of knowledge.

---

# ExternalEntity

An ExternalEntity represents an entity existing independently from DAA.

Examples:

* companies,
* professionals,
* institutions,
* organizations,
* communities.

At this stage the system does not assume participation.

It only recognizes existence.

---

# DiscoveryRecord

A DiscoveryRecord represents the observation event.

It connects:

```text
ExternalEntity

+

Discovery Context

=

Ecosystem Memory
```

A DiscoveryRecord may include:

* discovery source,
* date,
* evidence,
* classification,
* validation status,
* notes.

---

# Validation

Validation increases confidence in ecosystem knowledge.

Validation may include:

* information verification,
* source confirmation,
* relationship confirmation,
* administrator review.

Validation does not create identity.

It increases coherence of recognition.

---

# Recognition

When sufficient information exists, an ExternalEntity may become an Actor.

```text
ExternalEntity

↓

Recognized Identity

↓

Actor
```

Recognition means:

> This entity has a coherent identity within the ecosystem context.

---

# Voluntary Entry Path

Not every Participant begins through discovery.

An entity may directly approach DAA.

```text
External Entity

↓

Voluntary Integration

↓

Participant
```

Discovery and participation are different relationships.

---

# Evolution of Knowledge

Discovery information is not static.

As relationships evolve:

```text id="v5n8qa"
Discovery

↓

Recognition

↓

Profile Evolution

↓

New Understanding
```

The ecosystem continuously improves its knowledge.

---

# AI Role

AI amplifies Discovery capabilities.

It can support:

* information gathering,
* classification,
* relationship detection,
* pattern recognition,
* opportunity identification.

AI increases the capacity to understand the ecosystem.

Human judgment preserves meaning.

---

# Guiding Principle

> Discovery is the process through which reality becomes understandable without losing its original identity.

```text
Observe.

Recognize.

Relate.

Evolve.
```
