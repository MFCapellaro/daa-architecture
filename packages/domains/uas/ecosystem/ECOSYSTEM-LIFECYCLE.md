# DAA UAS Ecosystem Lifecycle

## Purpose

The Ecosystem Lifecycle describes the evolutionary path through which an external reality becomes a coherent participant within DAA.

The lifecycle preserves identity while allowing relationships to evolve.

---

# Fundamental Principle

> Evolution preserves identity while expanding coherent relationships.

An entity does not become different by entering DAA.

Its capacity to relate coherently expands.

---

# Lifecycle

```text
External Reality

↓

Observed

↓

Discovered

↓

Recognized

↓

Contextualized

↓

Integrated

↓

Participant

↓

Trajectory

↓

Evolution
```

Each stage represents an increase in relationship coherence.

---

# Stage 1 — External Reality

The entity exists independently of DAA.

DAA neither creates nor modifies its existence.

Question:

> What already exists?

---

# Stage 2 — Observed

The entity becomes visible.

Observation precedes classification.

Question:

> What have we perceived?

---

# Stage 3 — Discovered

The observation is preserved through a DiscoveryRecord.

Context, source and evidence become part of system memory.

Question:

> What do we know about it?

---

# Stage 4 — Recognized

The entity acquires a coherent identity within the ecosystem.

An Actor is recognized.

Recognition does not imply participation.

Question:

> Who is this entity within the ecosystem?

---

# Stage 5 — Contextualized

The Actor expresses its current capabilities.

ActorProfile represents:

* services,
* competencies,
* regions,
* operational context,
* current expression.

Identity remains stable.

Expression evolves.

Question:

> How is this Actor currently expressed?

---

# Stage 6 — Integrated

The Actor voluntarily establishes a formal relationship with DAA.

Integration is represented through IntegrationRecord.

Participation begins only after voluntary acceptance.

Question:

> Does this Actor wish to participate?

---

# Stage 7 — Participant

The entity becomes an internal participant of DAA.

The Participant can now:

* establish relationships,
* follow trajectories,
* contribute to ecosystem coherence.

Question:

> How does this identity contribute?

---

# Stage 8 — Trajectory

Participation generates experience.

Experience generates knowledge.

Knowledge generates coherence.

Each trajectory preserves the history of transformation.

Question:

> How is the participant evolving?

---

# Stage 9 — Evolution

Evolution is not accumulation.

Evolution is the coherent expansion of identity through relationships.

As coherence grows:

* opportunities emerge,
* collaboration expands,
* ecosystem potential becomes expressible.

Question:

> What new possibilities have emerged?

---

# Identity Through Time

```text
Identity

↓

Relationships

↓

Expression

↓

Trajectory

↓

Evolution
```

Identity remains.

Relationships transform.

Evolution emerges.

---

# AI Role

AI accompanies every stage of the lifecycle.

It amplifies the capacity to:

* observe,
* discover,
* recognize,
* contextualize,
* relate,
* learn.

AI never replaces voluntary participation.

Human agency remains the origin of every relationship.

---

# Emergent Ecosystem

As participants interact coherently, the ecosystem develops collective identity.

When sufficient coherence is reached, new capabilities naturally emerge, including:

* collective knowledge,
* regional interoperability,
* ecosystem metrics,
* representative capacity.

These capabilities are harvested, not imposed.

---

# Guiding Statement

> There is a time to plant and a time to harvest.

DAA plants coherent relationships.

Evolution harvests their expressed potential.
