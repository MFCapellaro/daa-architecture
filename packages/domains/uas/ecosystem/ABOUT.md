# UAS Ecosystem

## About

The UAS Ecosystem domain represents the existing context where unmanned aerial systems, their capabilities, actors, knowledge and relationships coexist.

It is the starting point for DAA interaction with reality.

DAA does not create the ecosystem.

DAA observes, recognizes and enables coherent relationships among the capabilities already present in the environment.

---

## Purpose

The purpose of the UAS Ecosystem domain is to make visible the existing conditions of the UAS context:

* organizations,
* suppliers,
* manufacturers,
* operators,
* professionals,
* services,
* knowledge,
* regional capabilities,
* opportunities.

The ecosystem is the field where potential relationships can be discovered and cultivated.

---

## Context Dependency

An ecosystem is not an isolated container of entities.

An ecosystem emerges from:

* a specific context,
* existing entities,
* environmental conditions,
* possible relationships,
* shared meaning.

Therefore, the UAS Ecosystem exists because the UAS context provides a unique combination of capabilities and relationships.

---

## Ecosystem Evolution

The ecosystem evolves through progressive relationship recognition:

```
Existing Conditions

↓

Observed Entities

↓

Potential Relationships

↓

Potential Actors

↓

DAA Actors

↓

Evolving Trajectories
```

An entity becomes an actor when a coherent relationship is established.

---

## Relationship with DAA

DAA provides the conditions for existing relationships to express their potential.

Within the UAS Ecosystem:

* DAA creates visibility and structure,
* relationships generate value,
* participants and suppliers develop trajectories,
* knowledge supports collective capability.

---

## Relationship with AI

AI amplifies the ability to recognize, connect and evolve potential relationships.

AI supports the ecosystem by:

* discovering patterns,
* organizing information,
* identifying opportunities,
* suggesting coherent connections,
* accelerating implementation.

AI does not replace relationships.

It increases the ecosystem's capacity to perceive and cultivate them.

---

## Relationship with Pool

The Pool domain is an initial mechanism for transforming ecosystem potential into active relationships.

The UAS Ecosystem answers:

> What exists?

The Pool answers:

> How can existing capabilities be connected coherently?

Together they create the foundation for an adaptive ecosystem.

---

## Core Principle

> DAA begins by recognizing what already exists, not by imposing what should exist.

The ecosystem is the soil.

Relationships are the roots.

Capabilities are the growth.

Coherent evolution is the outcome.
