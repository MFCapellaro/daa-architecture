# CIACs

## Purpose

Represents Civil Aviation Training Centers authorized by ANAC
to provide aeronautical training within the applicable regulatory
framework.

## Role

CIACs are ecosystem organizations that provide training and
support the qualification pathway of aeronautical personnel.

Within the RPA/RPAS ecosystem, CIACs may provide training
associated with remote pilot competence.
