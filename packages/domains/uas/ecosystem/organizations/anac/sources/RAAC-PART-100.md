# RAAC Part 100

## Identification

**Organization:** Administración Nacional de Aviación Civil (ANAC)

**Regulation:** Regulaciones Argentinas de Aviación Civil — Parte 100

**Title:** Requisitos Generales para la Operación de Aeronaves Pilotadas a Distancia (RPA) y de Sistemas de Aeronaves Pilotadas a Distancia (RPAS)

## Origin

The current Part 100 was approved within the regulatory framework established by Resolution ANAC 550/2025.

It corresponds to the Second Edition of Part 100.

## Purpose

Establishes general requirements applicable to the operation of RPA and RPAS.

Part 100 provides the general regulatory framework upon which the particular operational provisions of Parts 101 and 102 are built.

## Regulatory role

Part 100 establishes general concepts and requirements concerning:

- RPA/RPAS operations;
- operational categories;
- exclusions;
- general operational rules;
- limitations of operation;
- applicable operational conditions.

## Operational categories

Part 100 establishes the framework for operational categories applicable to RPA/RPAS.

Part 101 contains the particular provisions for the Open Category.

Part 102 contains the particular provisions for the Specific Category.

## Versioning

The Second Edition of Part 100 approved in 2025 was subsequently amended by Resolution 312/2026.

The current representation of Part 100 must therefore preserve both:

- the original Second Edition;
- subsequent amendments.

## Relevance to product relationships

Part 100 should not be represented as a static characteristic of an aircraft.

Regulatory applicability emerges from the relationship between the aircraft, its characteristics and the operational context.

Relevant relationships may include:

- aircraft → operational category;
- aircraft → operational limitations;
- aircraft → registration requirements;
- pilot → qualification requirements;
- operator → authorization requirements;
- operation → applicable conditions.
