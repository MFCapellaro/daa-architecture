# Resolución ANAC 312/2026

## Identification

**Organization:** Administración Nacional de Aviación Civil (ANAC)

**Instrument:** Resolución 312/2026

**Resolution identifier:** RESOL-2026-312-APN-ANAC#MEC

**Date of sanction:** 2026-05-21

**Date of publication:** 2026-05-22

**Official publication:** Boletín Oficial de la República Argentina, N.º 35915, página 19.

## Purpose

Aprueba la Enmienda N.º 1 a la Segunda Edición de la RAAC Parte 100.

## Scope of amendment

The amendment affects:

- Definitions.
- Section 100.03 — Exclusions.
- Section 100.04 — Operational Categories with RPA/RPAS.
- Section 100.17 — Operational Limitations.

The affected sections belong to Subpart A — Generalities and Subpart B — General Rules of Operation in Airspace.

## Relevant operational change

The amendment expands the Open Category for certain rotary-wing aircraft operating exclusively in rural areas.

This change removes previously applicable weight restrictions for the qualifying rural rotary-wing operations.

The modification has particular relevance to agricultural and rural applications of RPA/RPAS.

## Regulatory context

The amendment modifies the framework established by Resolution 550/2025 and its Second Edition of RAAC Part 100.

The change demonstrates that operational applicability cannot be inferred permanently from a product attribute alone.

Applicable requirements depend on the relationship between:

- aircraft characteristics;
- aircraft configuration;
- operational category;
- operational environment;
- location;
- type of operation;
- applicable regulation.

## Historical significance

This resolution represents a subsequent evolution of the regulatory framework established in 2025.

The previous framework must therefore remain traceable rather than being overwritten.
