# RAAC Part 102

## Identification

**Organization:** Administración Nacional de Aviación Civil (ANAC)

**Regulation:** Regulaciones Argentinas de Aviación Civil — Parte 102

**Title:** Disposiciones Particulares para la Operación de Aeronaves Pilotadas a Distancia (RPA) y de Sistemas de Aeronaves Pilotadas a Distancia (RPAS) — Categoría Específica

## Origin

Part 102 was approved by Resolution ANAC 550/2025.

## Purpose

Establishes particular operational provisions applicable to RPA/RPAS operating within the Specific Category.

## Operational concept

The Specific Category addresses operations with greater operational complexity or risk than those covered by the Open Category.

The category operates under a framework that requires evaluation of operational risk and corresponding mitigation measures.

## Operational authorization

Operations falling within the Specific Category are subject to the applicable authorization requirements established by ANAC.

The regulatory process may require:

- operational risk assessment;
- mitigation measures;
- pilot qualification;
- aircraft registration;
- operator requirements;
- operational authorization;
- other requirements established by the applicable regulation.

## Pilot relationship

Pilot requirements should be represented as a relationship between the pilot, the operation and the applicable regulatory conditions.

They should not be represented solely as a permanent characteristic of a product.

## Regulatory relationship

Part 102 must be interpreted together with:

- RAAC Part 100;
- applicable provisions concerning pilot certification;
- aircraft identification and registration requirements;
- operator requirements;
- applicable operational conditions.

## Regulatory evolution

Part 102 was established as part of the framework approved by Resolution 550/2025.

Future amendments must be represented as new regulatory evidence or versions rather than overwriting the historical source.

## Relevance to ecosystem

Part 102 provides the regulatory basis for representing operations that require specific operational treatment and authorization.
