# Resolución ANAC 550/2025

## Identification

**Organization:** Administración Nacional de Aviación Civil (ANAC)

**Instrument:** Resolución 550/2025

**Date of sanction:** 2025-08-07

**Date of publication:** 2025-08-08

**Official publication:** Boletín Oficial de la República Argentina, N.º 35724, página 14.

## Purpose

Establece un nuevo marco normativo para la operación de aeronaves pilotadas a distancia (RPA) y sistemas de aeronaves pilotadas a distancia (RPAS).

La resolución aprueba una nueva edición de la Parte 100 de las Regulaciones Argentinas de Aviación Civil (RAAC) y aprueba las Partes 101 y 102, estableciendo disposiciones generales y particulares para las operaciones de RPA/RPAS.

## Regulatory structure

The resolution establishes:

- RAAC Part 100 — General requirements for the operation of RPA/RPAS.
- RAAC Part 101 — Particular provisions for the Open Category.
- RAAC Part 102 — Particular provisions for the Specific Category.

The resolution also renumbers the previous RAAC Part 101, which concerned captive balloons, kites, amateur rockets and unmanned free balloons, as RAAC Part 32.

## Regulatory approach

The framework adopts an operational risk-based structure.

The operational categories established through the new framework are:

- Open Category.
- Specific Category.
- Certified Category, within the overall regulatory framework.

Part 101 establishes particular provisions for the Open Category.

Part 102 establishes particular provisions for the Specific Category.

## Historical effect

The resolution abrogates Resolutions 880/2019, 885/2019, 319/2025 and 334/2025.

Resolution 319/2025 had previously approved a Part 100, but it was subsequently abrogated by Resolution 550/2025.

## Relationships

This source establishes the regulatory framework subsequently amended by Resolution 312/2026.

## Relevance to ecosystem

This resolution is a primary regulatory source for determining operational requirements applicable to RPA/RPAS in Argentina.

It provides the basis for relating:

- aircraft;
- operators;
- remote pilots;
- operational categories;
- operational conditions;
- registration and identification requirements;
- authorization and certification requirements.
