# RAAC Part 101

## Identification

**Organization:** Administración Nacional de Aviación Civil (ANAC)

**Regulation:** Regulaciones Argentinas de Aviación Civil — Parte 101

**Title:** Disposiciones Particulares para la Operación de Aeronaves Pilotadas a Distancia (RPA) y de Sistemas de Aeronaves Pilotadas a Distancia (RPAS) — Categoría Abierta

## Origin

Part 101 was approved by Resolution ANAC 550/2025.

## Purpose

Establishes particular operational provisions applicable to RPA/RPAS operating within the Open Category.

## Operational concept

The Open Category is intended for operations with lower operational risk that meet the applicable conditions and limitations.

Operations within this category do not generally require prior operational authorization when the conditions established by the regulation are satisfied.

## Operational limitations

The Open Category is subject to operational limitations established by the applicable regulatory framework.

Examples include restrictions related to:

- prohibited, restricted or dangerous airspace;
- control zones;
- designated no-fly areas;
- emergency operations;
- other limitations established by the regulation.

## Pilot requirements

Pilot requirements depend on the applicable operational conditions and the current regulatory framework.

The regulatory representation should therefore preserve the relationship between:

- operation;
- category;
- aircraft;
- pilot;
- location;
- applicable requirements.

## Regulatory evolution

The Open Category framework was established through Resolution 550/2025.

Resolution 312/2026 subsequently amended Part 100 and expanded the Open Category for qualifying rotary-wing aircraft operating exclusively in rural areas.

This means that the applicability of the Open Category must not be reduced to a fixed aircraft class or weight attribute.

## Relevance to ecosystem

Part 101 is relevant for determining whether a particular operation can be conducted within the Open Category and which operational conditions apply.
