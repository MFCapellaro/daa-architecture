# Aircraft Identification Marking

## Purpose

Represents the identification marking assigned to an aircraft within the Argentine civil aviation registry.

## Current Identification Scheme

The current aircraft identification scheme assigns an identification
marking to registered aircraft within the Argentine civil aviation
registry.

For RPAS aircraft, the current registration scheme uses the `LV-R`
prefix.

Historical VNT/ARVNT registrations remain relevant as historical
regulatory evidence and must be preserved separately from the current
identification scheme.

## Principle

The identification marking belongs to the registered aircraft instance.

It must not be stored as a permanent characteristic of the commercial
product model.

## Product Relationship

A product model may correspond to multiple registered aircraft instances.

Each registered instance may therefore have its own regulatory identity.

## Historical Evolution

The identification system has evolved under the current regulatory framework.

The corpus must preserve the applicable historical source and the current identification scheme rather than overwriting historical evidence.
