# Operator Identification

## Purpose

Represents the regulatory identity of the person or organization responsible for an RPA/RPAS operation.

## Principle

The operator is an ecosystem participant.

Operator identity must therefore remain separate from:

- aircraft identity;
- product identity;
- pilot identity.

## Relationship

Operator
→ operates
→ Aircraft
→ Product

Pilot
→ operates / controls
→ Aircraft

These relationships may coexist without collapsing the identities of the participants.

## Regulatory Context

Applicable operator identification and documentation requirements depend on the current ANAC regulatory framework and operational category.
