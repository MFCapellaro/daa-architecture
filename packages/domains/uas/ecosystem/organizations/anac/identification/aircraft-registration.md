# Aircraft Registration

## Purpose

Represents the regulatory identification and registration of RPA/RPAS.

## Principle

Registration identifies an aircraft within the regulatory system.

It does not describe the semantic identity of the commercial product.

## Relationship

Product
→ Aircraft
→ Registration

The registered aircraft is therefore a regulatory instance of a product, rather than the product itself.

## Regulatory Context

Registration requirements depend on the applicable regulatory framework and operational category.

## Source

ANAC — Registro Nacional de Aeronaves.
