# Remote Pilot Certification

## Purpose

Represents the regulatory requirements applicable to the competence and authorization of a remote pilot.

## Regulatory Basis

ANAC regulates pilot licenses and certificates of competence through RAAC Part 61.

ANAC provides specific requirements for the Certificate of Competence of Remote Pilot for VANT/SVANT.

## Requirements

Requirements may include:

- minimum age;
- language requirements;
- required education;
- applicable aeronautical medical certification;
- approved theoretical and practical instruction;
- training through an ANAC-certified CIAC;
- theoretical examination;
- practical examination.

## Product Relationship

Pilot certification is not a characteristic of the aircraft.

It is a requirement of the relationship:

Pilot
→ Operation
→ Aircraft
→ Applicable regulatory conditions

The required pilot qualification may therefore depend on the characteristics and operational conditions of the operation.

## Historical Note

Previous VANT/SVANT regulations expressed pilot competence through categories and classes.

The current regulatory framework must be evaluated against the latest edition and amendments of RAAC Part 61.
