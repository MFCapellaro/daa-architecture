# Aeronautical Medical Certification

## Purpose

Represents the medical certification requirement associated with aeronautical personnel.

## Relationship to Remote Pilots

The medical certification applicable to a remote pilot is determined by the requirements established by the current regulatory framework.

Medical certification must therefore be represented as a regulatory requirement associated with the pilot and the applicable operation.

## Separation from Product

Medical certification is not a characteristic of an RPA/RPAS product.

It belongs to the pilot and operational regulatory context.

## Regulatory Dependency

This requirement is governed by the applicable edition of RAAC Part 67 and the corresponding provisions of RAAC Part 61.
