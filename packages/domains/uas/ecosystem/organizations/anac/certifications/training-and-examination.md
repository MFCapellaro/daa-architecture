# Remote Pilot Training and Examination

## Purpose

Represents the training and examination pathway required for remote pilot competence.

## Training

Training for remote pilots is conducted through a CIAC certified by ANAC when required by the applicable regulatory framework.

The instructional program must comply with the requirements established by ANAC.

## Examination

The certification process may include:

- theoretical examination;
- practical examination;
- verification by an ANAC-designated inspector.

## CIAC Relationship

The training relationship is:

Remote Pilot
→ receives training
→ CIAC
→ certified by ANAC
→ under applicable RAAC requirements

## Ecosystem Relationship

CIACs are ecosystem organizations.

They should therefore be represented independently from the pilot and independently from the aircraft product.
