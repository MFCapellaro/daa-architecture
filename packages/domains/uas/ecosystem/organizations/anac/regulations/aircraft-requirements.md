# Aircraft Requirements

## Purpose

Represents regulatory requirements applicable to an RPA/RPAS as an aircraft.

## Requirements

Depending on the applicable operational category and conditions, requirements may include:

- aircraft identification;
- registration;
- registration marking;
- applicable operational limitations;
- applicable authorization;
- applicable certification requirements.

## Separation from Product Characteristics

Regulatory requirements are not product characteristics.

A product may have:

- weight;
- payload;
- dimensions;
- endurance;
- propulsion;
- configuration;

while regulatory requirements determine whether and how that product may legally operate.

## Relationship

Product
→ Aircraft
→ Applicable regulatory requirements
