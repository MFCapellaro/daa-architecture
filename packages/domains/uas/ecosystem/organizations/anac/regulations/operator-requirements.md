# Operator Requirements

## Purpose

Represents requirements applicable to the person or organization responsible for an RPA/RPAS operation.

## Requirements

Depending on the applicable operation, requirements may include:

- registration;
- operational authorization;
- compliance with applicable limitations;
- documentation;
- safety and operational procedures.

## Principle

Operator requirements belong to the operational relationship and must not be represented as characteristics of the aircraft.
