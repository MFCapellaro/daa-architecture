# Operational Conditions

## Purpose

Represents the conditions that determine the regulatory applicability of an RPA/RPAS operation.

## Conditions

Relevant conditions may include:

- aircraft characteristics;
- maximum take-off mass;
- aircraft configuration;
- type of operation;
- operational environment;
- geographic location;
- airspace;
- operating limitations;
- applicable operational category.

## Context Dependency

Regulatory applicability must not be inferred exclusively from a product characteristic.

The same product may be subject to different operational requirements depending on the context in which it is operated.

## Rural Operations

The 2026 amendment to RAAC Part 100 introduced specific treatment for qualifying rotary-wing operations conducted exclusively in rural areas.

This demonstrates that operational context can modify the regulatory relationship applicable to an aircraft.
