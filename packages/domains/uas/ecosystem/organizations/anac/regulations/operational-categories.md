# Operational Categories

## Purpose

Represents the operational categories established by the current ANAC regulatory framework for RPA/RPAS.

This document is a semantic representation derived from regulatory sources.

It does not replace the originating regulation.

## Categories

### Open Category

The Open Category applies to operations that satisfy the conditions and operational limitations established by the applicable regulations.

Operations within this category generally do not require prior operational authorization when all applicable conditions are satisfied.

The applicability of the Open Category depends on the aircraft and the operational context.

### Specific Category

The Specific Category applies to operations requiring specific operational treatment because their characteristics or circumstances exceed the conditions established for the Open Category.

Operations in this category are subject to the applicable authorization and risk-management requirements.

### Certified Category

The Certified Category applies to operations subject to the certification framework established by the applicable regulations.

Its requirements are distinct from those applicable to Open and Specific operations.

## Important Principle

Operational category is not a permanent characteristic of a product.

The applicable category emerges from the relationship between:

- aircraft;
- aircraft characteristics;
- operation;
- operational environment;
- location;
- applicable regulatory conditions.
