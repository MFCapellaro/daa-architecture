# UAS Domain

## Purpose

The UAS Domain organizes the agricultural drone ecosystem through coherent knowledge, observable relationships and collective opportunities.

It does not create the ecosystem.

It reveals, organizes and enables its coherent evolution.

---

## Core Principle

Reality precedes organization.

Organization precedes interaction.

Interaction reveals collective capability.
