# Individual Trajectory

## Purpose

Trajectory describes the evolution of an individual identity through relationships within the ecosystem.

A trajectory preserves identity while transforming through experience.

## Guiding Principle

The value of a system emerges when individual trajectories become coherent contributions to a shared ecosystem.

## Trajectory Model

```text
Identity

↓

Context

↓

Participation

↓

Interaction

↓

Experience

↓

Knowledge

↓

Contribution

↓

New Possibilities
```

## Participant Trajectory

A participant evolves through:

* declared interests,
* Pool participation,
* shared experiences,
* learning activities,
* service relationships,
* accumulated reputation.

## Supplier Trajectory

A supplier evolves through:

* published capabilities,
* Pool participation,
* operational performance,
* participant feedback,
* ecosystem reputation.
