# UAS Pool

## Purpose

UAS Pool is a DAA application that enables coherent collective purchasing by connecting organized demand with structured supply.

UAS Pool does not sell products or process payments.

UAS Pool enables relationships between participants and suppliers through transparent and traceable collective formations.
