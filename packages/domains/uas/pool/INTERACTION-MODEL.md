# Interaction Model

## Purpose

Interaction Model describes how relationships generate information, learning and new capabilities.

## Guiding Principle

Interactions are not isolated events. They are generators of trajectory.

## Interaction Flow

```text
Actor

↓

Relationship

↓

Interaction

↓

Experience

↓

Knowledge

↓

Trajectory Update
```

## Interaction Examples

A participant shares a solution.

The system transforms that interaction into:

* contextual knowledge,
* future recommendations,
* regional intelligence,
* improved opportunities.

A supplier receives feedback.

The system transforms it into:

* service improvement,
* reputation evolution,
* better future alignment.

## System Memory

Not every interaction becomes system memory.

Through discernment, relevant experiences become:

* knowledge,
* metrics,
* recommendations,
* future opportunities.

## Core Principle

The ecosystem grows by increasing coherence among individual trajectories.

The common good emerges when relationships increase collective capability.
