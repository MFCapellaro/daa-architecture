# UAS Pool Architecture

## Purpose

UAS Pool defines the architectural conditions through which collective purchasing emerges from coherent relationships between participants and suppliers.

UAS Pool transforms distributed demand into structured opportunities while preserving transparency, traceability, and relationship continuity.

---

# Architectural Boundaries

UAS Pool does not:

* Sell products
* Replace suppliers
* Process commercial payments
* Assign premature reputation values

UAS Pool does:

* Connect demand and supply
* Structure collective opportunities
* Preserve relationship context
* Enable transparent evolution
