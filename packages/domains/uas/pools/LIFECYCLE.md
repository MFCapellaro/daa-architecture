# Pool Lifecycle

## Purpose

The Pool lifecycle describes the evolution of a collective relationship from initial opportunity detection to the generation of new ecosystem capabilities.

A Pool is not only a purchasing mechanism. It is a dynamic structure that transforms individual interests into coherent collective trajectories.

## Guiding Principle

A dynamic system begins by observing existing conditions and relationships before defining the structures that will transform them.

The Pool emerges from real conditions:

* existing supplier capabilities,
* participant needs,
* geographic context,
* shared interests,
* operational opportunities.

## Lifecycle Stages

## 1. Discovery

A supplier capability or collective opportunity becomes visible.

Relationships begin through:

* product interest,
* geographic relevance,
* operational needs,
* complementary activities.

The system observes and connects existing conditions.

## 2. Formation

Participants express interest and begin forming a collective identity.

The Pool acquires:

* participants,
* supplier relationship,
* shared objective,
* expected conditions.

The possibility starts becoming a coherent structure.

## 3. Confirmation

The collective reaches sufficient coherence to become a committed trajectory.

The relationship evolves from interest to responsibility.

The system provides:

* transparency,
* visibility,
* communication,
* traceability.

## 4. Transaction

The collective operation is executed.

The transaction is an event inside the lifecycle, not the final objective.

## 5. Experience

The Pool generates knowledge through interaction.

Participants and suppliers contribute:

* experiences,
* observations,
* solutions,
* recommendations.

The Pool becomes a source of collective learning.

## 6. Evolution

The accumulated experience generates new possibilities:

* future Pools,
* services,
* training,
* regional capabilities,
* new relationships.

The system increases its sensitivity to the environment.

## Core Principle

The structure must grow at the same pace that relationships gain coherence.

The Pool evolves by preserving identity while expanding relationships.
