# Adaptation

## Purpose

Adaptation defines the coherent transformation through which systems integrate experience while preserving identity.

Adaptation does not replace identity.

Adaptation prepares reusable capability for evolutionary integration.

---

## Responsibility

Adaptation defines the conditions where coherent change becomes evolutionary progress.

It:

* preserves identity during transformation,
* integrates experience generated through emergence,
* modifies behavior according to coherent conditions,
* enables systems to integrate learning while preserving identity.

Adaptation transforms experience into coherent evolution.

---

## Identity

Identity provides the continuity that makes adaptation possible.

Without identity, transformation becomes alteration.

Adaptation preserves what must remain while transforming what can evolve.

---

## Concepts

Concepts define the elements of adaptive transformation.

The fundamental concepts are:

* Identity
* Coherence
* Experience
* Discernment
* Transformation
* Capability
* Evolution

## Precedence Laws

Precedence Laws define the order of coherent adaptation.

Fundamental laws:

* Identity precedes Transformation.
* Experience precedes Discernment.
* Discernment precedes Adaptation.
* Coherence precedes Evolution.
* Adaptation preserves Identity.

---

## Relationship with Generation

Generation creates emergent capability.

Emergent capability produces experience.

Experience enables adaptation.

Adaptation modifies future conditions of generation.

Generation and Adaptation form a continuous evolutionary cycle.

## Generative Plasticity

Generative Plasticity is the capacity of a system to modify its conditions of emergence while preserving coherence.

Adaptation provides the feedback mechanism through which generated capability influences future generation.

---

## Design Principles

Adaptation preserves identity through coherent transformation.

Adaptation integrates experience into enriched identity.

A system evolves when it can transform without losing coherence.
