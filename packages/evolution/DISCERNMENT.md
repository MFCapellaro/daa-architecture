# Discernment

## Purpose

Discernment defines the coherent recognition through which systems identify meaningful transformations.

Discernment does not create change.

Discernment reveals which changes preserve identity and increase capability.

---

## Responsibility

Discernment defines the conditions where experience becomes meaningful adaptation.

It:

* interprets emergent experience,
* identifies coherent transformation paths,
* preserves alignment with purpose,
* enables adaptation through meaningful decisions.

Discernment transforms experience into evolutionary direction.

---

## Identity

Identity provides the reference from which discernment evaluates transformation.

Without identity, change cannot be recognized as coherent.

Discernment preserves continuity by distinguishing what must remain from what can evolve.

---

## Concepts

Concepts define the elements of coherent recognition.

The fundamental concepts are:

* Identity
* Meaning
* Experience
* Context
* Coherence
* Purpose
* Adaptation

## Precedence Laws

Precedence Laws define the order of meaningful adaptation.

Fundamental laws:

* Identity precedes Discernment.
* Experience precedes Meaning.
* Meaning precedes Discernment.
* Discernment precedes Adaptation.
* Coherence guides Transformation.

---

## Relationship with Adaptation

Adaptation transforms the system.

Discernment guides the direction of transformation.

Without discernment, adaptation becomes reaction.

Without adaptation, discernment remains understanding without evolution.

Together they enable coherent evolution.

---

## Relationship with Generation

Generation creates emergent experience through capability.

Discernment interprets this experience.

The interpretation of experience modifies future conditions of generation.

Generation provides experience.

Discernment provides evolutionary direction.

Discernment preserves meaning through transformation.

Discernment enables adaptation without losing identity.

A system evolves when it can distinguish coherent change from incoherent change.
