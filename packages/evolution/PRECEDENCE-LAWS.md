# Evolution Precedence Laws

## Purpose

Precedence Laws define the order of conditions through which evolutionary capability emerges.

Precedence establishes the conditions where coherent transformation reveals new capability.

---

## Identity precedes Coherence

Identity provides continuity.

Coherence preserves the relationships that maintain identity through transformation.

```text
Identity
    ↓
Coherence
```

---

## Coherence precedes Discernment

Coherence establishes the relational condition through which discernment emerges.

Discernment identifies the relationships aligned with identity and equilibrium.

```text
Coherence
    ↓
Discernment
```

---

## Discernment precedes Potency

Discernment reveals the relationships containing evolutionary potential.

Potency emerges from coherent relationships recognized through discernment.

```text
Discernment
    ↓
Potency
```

---

## Potency precedes Emergence

Potency contains the capacity for new expression.

Emergence reveals the capability enabled by coherent transformation.

```text
Potency
    ↓
Emergence
```

---

## Emergence precedes Capability

Emergence makes new capability observable.

Capability represents the evolutionary expression of preserved coherence.

```text
Emergence
    ↓
Capability
```

---

## Complete Evolutionary Precedence

```text
Identity
    ↓
Coherence
    ↓
Discernment
    ↓
Potency
    ↓
Emergence
    ↓
Capability
```

---

## Generative Condition

Precedence establishes the conditions.

Emergence reveals the resulting capability.

Together, precedence and emergence form the generative relationship through which evolution occurs.
