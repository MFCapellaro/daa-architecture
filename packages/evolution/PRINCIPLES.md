# Evolution Principles

## Identity Preservation

Identity is preserved through coherent transformation.

Evolution increases capability while maintaining continuity.

---

## Coherence Discernment

Coherence is the relational filter through which discernment emerges.

Discernment identifies the relationships that preserve identity and equilibrium.

---

## Potency Revelation

Coherence reveals the potency contained in coherent relationships.

Potency emerges when relationships reach coherent dynamic equilibrium.

---

## Emergent Capability

Capability emerges from coherent transformation.

Evolution reveals the capabilities contained in sustained adaptive coherence.

---

## Generative Condition

Precedence establishes the conditions for emergence.

Emergence reveals the coherence of the preceding conditions.

Together, precedence and emergence form a generative condition.

---

## Evolutionary Flow

```text
Identity
    ↓
Coherence
    ↓
Discernment
    ↓
Potency
    ↓
Emergence
    ↓
Capability
```
