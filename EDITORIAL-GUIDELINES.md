# Editorial Guidelines

## Purpose

These guidelines preserve a consistent editorial voice across DAA documentation.

DAA writing reflects conceptual clarity, semantic continuity, and architectural coherence.

## Core Principles

- Grammar is the single source of meaning.
- Meaning precedes expression.
- Identity is preserved through evolution.
- Core consumes Grammar.
- Domain consumes Core.
- Conceptual Governance preserves semantic continuity.

## Writing Principles

- Prefer conceptual precision over stylistic flourish.
- Express architectural properties before architectural restrictions.
- Describe what the architecture is before describing what it prevents.
- Preserve semantic continuity across every document.
- Express implementation as an expression of the architecture, never as its source.
- Preserve terminology unless a conceptual revision is explicitly approved.

## Editorial Rules

- Grammar defines meaning.
- Downstream documents derive meaning from Grammar.
- Existing concepts are refined, never redefined.
- New concepts are introduced only through Grammar.
- Editorial refinement improves clarity while preserving meaning.

## Document Hierarchy

1. ABOUT.md establishes identity.
2. GRAMMAR.md defines meaning.
3. CONCEPTUAL-GOVERNANCE.md preserves semantic continuity.
4. EDITORIAL-GUIDELINES.md preserves editorial coherence.

## Revision Standard

Every editorial revision is validated against Grammar before adoption.

Refinements increase clarity, consistency, or expressive power while preserving meaning.

Changes that modify meaning are conceptual evolutions and follow Conceptual Governance.
